REGULATORY DISCLAIMER

FLIGHTMARK IS NOT A REGULATORY RECORD

Currency, certification, flight-time and duty displays are computed from what you have entered in this app. They are a convenience for keeping track — not a determination that you are legal to fly or to work.

WHAT OVERRIDES THIS APP

Your operator's operations specifications, its approved training programme, any FAA-approved deviation, your state licensing board, and your certifying bodies all take precedence over every default in this app. Where a programme is stricter than the regulation, the programme governs. Part 135 pilots are held to 135.247 and their operator's programme rather than 61.57 (61.57(e)(3)); the app labels them that way, but can't see the programme.

WHAT THIS APP CANNOT SEE

Some requirements cannot be derived from a logbook at all. The app does not know about check rides you have not entered, letters of authorisation, medical conditions, duty limits imposed by your operator, commercial flying you didn't log here, or anything recorded only in your company's system.

NIGHT VISION GOGGLES

HNVGO status compares the operations you logged with the requirement you set for your programme. It is not a determination under 61.57(f) or your operator's NVG programme, which also require tasks this app doesn't track. Night time is not assumed to be goggle time.

NIGHT AND CROSS-COUNTRY TIME

Night time is computed from civil twilight (14 CFR 1.1) when a flight has departure and arrival times, and is otherwise what you enter. Cross-country time is logged automatically when a landing is more than 25 nm (helicopter) or 50 nm (airplane) from the departure point, the distances 61.1 uses for certificate experience; you can change it on any flight.

FLIGHT-TIME AND DUTY LIMITS

The Part 135 figures count only flights and shifts logged here. Flights without departure and arrival times are counted in full against the 24-hour limit. Your operator's records are what the FAA will look at.

BADGES

Achievement badges are a record of what you have logged. They are not qualifications and carry no regulatory meaning.

Confirm your status with your chief pilot, clinical director, or the appropriate authority. Do not rely on this app alone.
