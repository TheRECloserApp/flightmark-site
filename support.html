FlightMark

Support

FlightMark is a personal flight logbook for air medical crews and pilots, published by Dalton Underwood.

For help with the app, a question about your data, or to report another person's Friends profile, write to flightmarkapp@icloud.com.

Your logbook is stored on your device and, if you use iCloud, in your own private iCloud. We cannot see it or restore it for you; use Backup in Settings to keep a copy.

Airport data comes from OurAirports, released into the public domain. Every source is listed under Attributions & Licences.
